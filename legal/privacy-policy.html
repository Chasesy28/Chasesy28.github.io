Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Silly Site collects, uses, and shares information when you use this website and related admin features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Basic usage data: browser/device data and technical logs generated when pages load.
  • Local storage data: theme, accessibility preferences, announcement dismissal state, and cookie consent choice.
  • Admin authentication data: for admin access, Google OAuth and account data are processed through Supabase Auth.
  • Admin content data: admin-created announcements and related metadata are stored in Supabase.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate and secure the website and admin area.
  • Remember user settings (theme/accessibility/consent).
  • Display and manage announcements.
  • Troubleshoot and improve site reliability.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use essential browser storage for core functionality. We also request consent for optional storage via the cookie banner. You can remove stored values any time from your browser site-data settings.

4. Supabase and Service Providers

Supabase is used for authentication and database features (including admin users and announcements). Supabase may process data according to its own terms, security controls, and regional infrastructure settings.

Other service providers may include infrastructure/CDN providers and external asset hosts (for example, fonts and JS/CDN resources).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers solely to run site functionality, enforce security, and support operations.

6. Data Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed for operations, legal obligations, and security needs. Admin/auth records and announcements may be retained until deleted or no longer needed.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. To request this, contact us through the repository issue tracker.

8. Security

We use technical controls including Supabase authentication capabilities, access controls, and transport security practices. No method of storage/transmission is guaranteed 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves toward production deployment. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at GitHub Issues.
